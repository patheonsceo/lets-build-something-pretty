# Phase 6: low-fidelity design

## Goal
Every section laid out, explained and signed off in low fidelity, then a
frozen shot list for Phase 7. Mistakes caught here cost minutes; after the
build they cost days.

## Steps
1. **Say it is low fidelity.** Tell the user plainly: "This is a low-fidelity
   design: grey boxes, real copy, motion written in captions. It is about
   layout, flow and concept, not polish." Put a LOW-FI label on every board.
2. **One section at a time**, in page order. For each board:
   - the layout on the grid, with the real copy from the brief
   - where the theme's layers and light are at that point
   - the motion, in captions (what moves, on scroll or hover, for how long)
   - the concept behind the section in 1-2 lines
   - interactions (hover, tabs, pins) and the phone version
   Gate each section. Iterate on "not quite".
3. **Hero moments** get a frame strip (4-6 frames) showing the scroll story.
4. **Critique pass** before the final gate. Review every section with a
   designer's eye (`impeccable` critique or gstack `design-review` if
   installed): which sections are weaker than the rest, feel templated, or
   break the theme? Present the findings with fixes. Redo the weak ones.
5. **Freeze the shot list.** Fill `.pretty/shotlist.md`: every image, video
   and frame sequence each section needs, with size, aspect and prompt.
   Gate: "Layout + shot list approved?"

## Red flags
- Polished hi-fi mockups presented as low-fi (the user judges polish, not layout)
- Lorem ipsum instead of the client's copy
- Skipping the critique pass because "the user approved each section"
- Starting asset generation before the shot list is frozen
