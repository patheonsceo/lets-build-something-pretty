# Phase 1: finding the direction

## Goal
Agree on where the site is going (the feel, the references, the concept)
by researching from both sides and iterating until the user approves one.

## Steps
1. **Ask the user for inspiration** (plain text): sites, brands, posters,
   films, anything they love, and anything they hate. Ask why for each.
2. **Research yourself.** Use web search plus the gstack browser; look, do not
   guess. Cover at least 4 sources, e.g. Awwwards, Godly, Siteinspire,
   Dribbble, Behance, Pinterest, and 2-3 competitors or category leaders.
   Screenshot what you find and Read the screenshots. Keep a list of links
   with one line each on what is worth stealing (layout, motion, type, mood).
3. **Open the user's references** in the browser and look at them the same way.
4. **Synthesize 2-4 distinct directions.** Each: a name, a one-line concept,
   a moodboard board (screenshots + notes), what it would feel like for this
   brand's audience, and the risk. Recommend one and say why.
   Put them on the canvas (`references/canvas.md`).
5. **Gate** with the picker. "Not quite" is a verdict: record what missed,
   combine or redo, gate again. Loop until one direction is approved.
6. Record the approved direction and the rejected ones (with reasons).

## Skills and tools
gstack browse (screenshots), WebSearch, `superpowers:brainstorming` if
installed, the canvas.

## Red flags
- Describing a reference you did not open
- One direction presented as "the plan"
- Directions that differ only in colour
- Moving to the design system before the direction gate closes
