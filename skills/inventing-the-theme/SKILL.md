# Phase 5: inventing the theme

## Goal
One idea that runs through the whole site and makes it unforgettable, while
the design system lays on top of it. This is where a good site becomes one
people show off. Be ambitious; the user can always pull it back.

## What a theme is
Not a colour scheme. A theme answers all five:
1. **The through-line.** One idea the whole page tells (e.g. "one sample
   over 24 hours", "one litre of water from intake to outfall", "a day in
   the kitchen from 4am proofing to last service").
2. **How sections flow.** How backgrounds, light and space change from
   section to section so there are no flat blocks (e.g. a continuous
   time-of-day gradient, water clearing as you scroll).
3. **Site-wide layers.** What persists on every screen (a grid, a ruler, a
   clock, grain, a cursor, a line that runs down the page).
4. **Motion language.** How things move and why it fits the idea (scrubbed
   camera moves, pinned stories, particles that become data, reveals).
5. **Hero moments.** 1-3 full-screen cinematic moments and exactly what the
   user sees while scrolling through each (frame by frame, in words or a
   frame strip).

## Steps
1. Re-read the brief, direction and design system. Research motion references
   in the gstack browser (Awwwards "Sites of the Day", Godly) and look at them.
2. Brainstorm widely (`superpowers:brainstorming` if installed), then propose
   **at least 3 genuinely different themes**, each answering all five points,
   plus: why it fits this audience, what it costs (assets, video, WebGL,
   performance risk) and a reduced-motion version. Mark a recommendation.
3. Show each on the canvas: a vertical strip of the whole page (how the light
   and sections flow) and a frame strip for each hero moment.
4. Gate. Themes may be combined ("1 + 3"). Iterate until one is chosen.
5. Record the theme, its hero moments and their asset needs in `decisions.md`.

## Rationalizations
| Thought | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Corporate clients want something safe" | They want credible. Credible and cinematic are not opposites; the theme carries the credibility. |
| "Hero, features, testimonials, CTA is proven" | That is the template the user is paying to escape. |
| "One signature moment is enough" | Offer it as one option among 3; let the user choose restraint. |
| "Too expensive to build" | Say the cost; do not pre-reject the idea. |

## Red flags
- Themes that are just colour or font changes
- Only one theme offered
- No reduced-motion answer
- Hero moments described as "a nice animation" without frames
